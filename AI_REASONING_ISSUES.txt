   - This is CORRECT behaviour — early starters should avoid the deadline day
   - To verify gap detection works: retest Task B with neutral/deadline-driven personality
   - Possible enhancement: add "natural gap bonus" (+10-15) when slot fits between
     two scheduled tasks within 30min buffer — makes tight gaps more competitive
